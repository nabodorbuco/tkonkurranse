The Event

The Trøndelagkonkurrans is a mathematical competition for students in Trøndelag. Its target group is students in 8, 9 and 10. trinn (Level I), and Vg1 and Vg2 (Level II).

The competition consists of three rounds: each one with two examinations of a duration of 1 hour and 15 minutes. Click on Problems to see what to expect from each part. Each is graded from 0 to 10, 10 being a full score.
