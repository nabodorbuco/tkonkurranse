Example

Suppose that $a,b,c,d,e,f$ are real numbers. We have

$$ (a^2+b^2)^2(c^4+d^4)(e^4+f^4) \geq (ace+bdf)^4. $$

Proof

By Cauchy-Schwarz,

$$ (ace+bdf)^2 \leq (a^2+b^2)(c^2e^2+d^2f^2). $$

Applying the appropriate inequality to the second factor gives the desired result.
